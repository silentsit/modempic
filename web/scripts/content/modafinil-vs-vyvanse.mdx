Modafinil vs Vyvanse names two different drug classes, not two nootropics in the same one. Modafinil ([Provigil](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=e16c26ad-7bc2-d155-3a5d-da83ad6492c8)) is a Schedule IV wakefulness-promoting agent labeled for narcolepsy, obstructive sleep apnea, and shift work disorder.[1] Vyvanse ([lisdexamfetamine](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=704e4378-ca83-445c-8b45-3cfa51c1ecad)) is a Schedule II CNS stimulant labeled for ADHD in people 6 and older, and for moderate to severe binge eating disorder in adults.[2]

Lisdexamfetamine is a prodrug. After you swallow it, the body converts it to dextroamphetamine.[2] That is the same active amphetamine isomer that sits inside mixed-salt products such as Adderall, delivered on a once-daily prodrug clock. The mixed-salt comparison, including the 2024 Trotti sleep-disorder trial, is [modafinil vs Adderall](/blog/modafinil-vs-adderall). This page is the prodrug and binge-eating split.

This page is catalog and label education, not medical advice. Legal status varies by country.

## A prodrug is not a gentler class

The conversion step is real. It is not a new pharmacology.

Once lisdexamfetamine becomes dextroamphetamine, you are in amphetamine territory. Boxed warning for abuse, misuse, and addiction. Schedule II storage and refill rules.[2]

Modafinil's label still says the mechanism of wakefulness is unknown and groups some actions with sympathomimetic agents without calling the profiles identical.[1] That is not a claim that modafinil "is a mild Vyvanse."

Food delays dextroamphetamine Tmax after a Vyvanse capsule by about an hour. It does not wipe the AUC.[2] Modafinil's food effect is a similar delay of peak, about an hour on the Provigil label.[1]

## For ADHD, the network numbers go one way

Modafinil has no FDA approval for ADHD at any age.[1] Vyvanse does, from age 6.[2]

Cortese and colleagues pooled 133 randomized ADHD trials.[3] In adults, amphetamines beat placebo on clinician-rated core symptoms (SMD −0.79). Modafinil did not (SMD 0.16, 95% CI −0.28 to 0.59).[3] Head-to-head comparisons in that network favored amphetamines over modafinil in children and adults.[3]

Stuhec's 2019 adult-ADHD meta-analysis included lisdexamfetamine as its own arm alongside mixed amphetamine salts, methylphenidate, and modafinil.[4] Modafinil again sat on a confidence interval that crossed zero (SMD 0.08, 95% CI −0.18 to 0.34), while methylphenidate showed a moderate effect.[4] That paper is why "modafinil vs Vyvanse for ADHD" is not a close call on current adult evidence.

## For sleep disorders, Vyvanse is not on the label

Vyvanse is not approved for narcolepsy or shift work.[2] Modafinil is approved for both, plus leftover OSA sleepiness with airway treatment still in place.[1]

The [AASM 2021 guideline](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34743789/) gives modafinil a strong recommendation for adult narcolepsy. Dextroamphetamine gets a conditional recommendation for narcolepsy only, and none for idiopathic hypersomnia.[5] Lisdexamfetamine is not the named agent in that recommendation set. Do not smuggle Vyvanse into a narcolepsy first-line story because it becomes d-amphetamine in plasma.

## Three people, same question, different answer

An adult with diagnosed ADHD is inside Vyvanse's label and outside modafinil's.[1][2] The network analysis is on the amphetamine side.[3]

An adult with moderate to severe binge eating disorder is inside a Vyvanse indication that modafinil does not have.[2] That is not a weight-loss license. The label says so.

A person with narcolepsy or shift work disorder is inside modafinil's label and outside Vyvanse's.[1][2] A college student without either diagnosis is outside both, and unprescribed Vyvanse is Schedule II.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is Vyvanse stronger than modafinil?

They are labeled for different jobs. For adult ADHD, amphetamines (the class Vyvanse belongs to after conversion) have a large effect in the 2018 network meta-analysis; modafinil does not.[2][3] For narcolepsy, modafinil is the labeled and guideline-preferred drug; Vyvanse is not labeled for it.[1][2][5]

### Can modafinil replace Vyvanse for ADHD?

Not on the label. Modafinil has no ADHD approval, and adult meta-analyses do not show a reliable effect over placebo.[1][3][4]

### Does Vyvanse last longer than Adderall?

That is a formulation question inside the amphetamine class, not a modafinil question. Vyvanse is once-daily; capsule Tmax for dextroamphetamine is about 3.5 hours fasted in the pediatric PK set on the label.[2] Immediate-release Adderall is a different schedule of peaks. See [modafinil vs Adderall](/blog/modafinil-vs-adderall) for the mixed-salt file.

### Is Vyvanse a nootropic?

The US label is ADHD and adult binge eating disorder. It is explicitly not a weight-loss drug.[2] Off-label cognitive enhancement in healthy adults is not an indication for either product.

### Is there a trial comparing modafinil and Vyvanse head to head?

Not in the file I used. The closest same-paper comparisons are class-level ADHD meta-analyses that include lisdexamfetamine or amphetamines and, separately, modafinil.[3][4]
