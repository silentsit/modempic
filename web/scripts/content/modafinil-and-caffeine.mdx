Can you mix modafinil and caffeine? The [Provigil label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=e16c26ad-7bc2-d155-3a5d-da83ad6492c8) does not name caffeine as a contraindicated pair, and it does not give a stacking dose.[1] The trials that put both names in the same paper almost always compared them as separate arms, not as one combined regimen.

No labeled combination exists. I did not find a dedicated randomized trial that dosed modafinil and caffeine together as the treatment of interest and then measured the mix against either drug alone. What exists is sleep-deprivation work from Walter Reed and later labs: caffeine 200 to 600 mg versus modafinil 200 to 400 mg, each against placebo, in people who had been kept awake.[2][3][4][5]

This page is catalog and label education, not medical advice. Legal status varies by country.

## The trials compared them separately

Most "modafinil and caffeine" pages treat a head-to-head sleep-loss trial as proof that the two belong in the same cup. That is the wrong read.

[Wesensten, Killgore, and Balkin](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16120100/) at Walter Reed compared caffeine 600 mg, dextroamphetamine 20 mg, and modafinil 400 mg during 85 hours of total sleep deprivation.[2] All three improved psychomotor vigilance speed and objective alertness versus placebo. At those doses they were equally effective for about 2 to 4 hours on simple performance and alertness. Executive-function results were mixed.[2]

Killgore and colleagues later gave the same three agents after 44 hours of continuous wakefulness.[3] Relative to placebo, all three restored psychomotor vigilance test speed and reduced lapses. Caffeine's duration of action was the shortest. Dextroamphetamine's was the longest. Recovery sleep was worse after dextroamphetamine; none of the three wrecked post-recovery performance.[3]

Wesensten's 2004 Aviation, Space, and Environmental Medicine paper compared modafinil and caffeine on fatigue during sleep deprivation.[4] Dagan and Doljansky later used lower, matched 200 mg doses of each during sustained wakefulness and reported equal effectiveness against the nocturnal drop in cognitive performance.[5]

Those designs answer "which single agent, at this dose, after this much wake?" They do not answer "what happens if you take both?"

## What the label says about CYP1A2

Caffeine is a CYP1A2 substrate. The Provigil label reports that, in vitro, modafinil weakly induces CYP1A2, CYP2B6, and possibly CYP3A in a concentration-related way.[1] That sentence is a hepatocyte finding. It is not a clinical instruction to raise or cut your coffee.

A 2018 cocktail interaction trial used 100 mg caffeine as the CYP1A2 probe around 200 mg modafinil.[7] After a single modafinil dose, the caffeine AUC ratio was 0.95 (95% CI 0.87 to 1.03). After seven days of 200 mg, the ratio was 0.88 (95% CI 0.73 to 1.07).[7] Both intervals cross 1.0. That is not a demonstrated in-vivo wipeout of caffeine clearance.

If the two collide, it is more likely two wake effects in one body than a large CYP crash.

## Additive wake, not a documented synergy

The Provigil label lists insomnia, nervousness, anxiety, and headache among common adverse reactions.[1] Caffeine can produce the same cluster: jitter, sleep disruption, a faster pulse.[6] The FDA notes wide person-to-person variation in caffeine sensitivity, and flags pregnancy, some medicines, and some conditions as reasons to ask a clinician about limits.[6] Rapid intake near 1,200 mg can reach toxic effects such as seizures.[6]

I am not going to invent a "safe stack" milligram pair. The label does not publish one. The sleep-deprivation arms used caffeine doses (200 to 600 mg) that already sit at or above a single strong coffee day for many people, given as a laboratory bolus, not as an all-day drip on top of 200 mg modafinil.[2][4][5]

If a prescriber has you on modafinil, leftover espresso is a clinical conversation, not a blog titration.

## Three people, same question, different file

A night-shift nurse on labeled shift-work dosing who also drinks coffee at 02:00 is stacking two wake signals on a 15-hour effective half-life.[1] The Walter Reed work says either agent can restore vigilance for a few hours after extreme sleep loss.[2] It does not say the pair is safer, longer, or smarter.

A student who is well slept and wants a "focus stack" is outside every US indication on the Provigil label.[1] Healthy-adult cognitive gains on modafinil alone are small and task-specific. That file lives on [what is modafinil](/blog/what-is-modafinil). Adding caffeine does not create an approval.

A patient on 200 mg for leftover OSA sleepiness who still has a morning coffee is the most ordinary case. The label still wants CPAP in place.[1] The cocktail trial does not show a large CYP1A2 crash.[7] Insomnia and a racing pulse are still on both sides of the table.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can you take modafinil and caffeine together?

There is no labeled combination and no dedicated co-administration RCT in the file I used.[1][2] Sleep-deprivation trials compared the two drugs as separate arms.[2][3][5] People do drink coffee on modafinil. That is not the same as evidence that the mix is studied, safe, or useful.

### Does caffeine cancel or boost modafinil?

I do not have a synergy trial. CYP1A2 probe data around 200 mg modafinil left caffeine AUC near unchanged.[7] Shared adverse-event lists (insomnia, nervousness, headache) are the more honest overlap.[1][6]

### Is caffeine as effective as modafinil when you have not slept?

At the high laboratory doses used at Walter Reed (caffeine 600 mg vs modafinil 400 mg), simple vigilance recovered about as well for a few hours.[2] At 200 mg versus 200 mg, Dagan reported equal rescue of the nocturnal performance drop.[5] Caffeine's action was shorter in the 44-hour Killgore comparison.[3] Those are sleep-loss designs, not ADHD or office-focus designs.

### How much caffeine is "too much" on its own?

The FDA cites 400 mg a day as an amount not generally associated with negative effects for most adults, with a lot of individual variation.[6] That figure is not a stack limit for people on modafinil.

### Does the Provigil label mention coffee?

Not as a named food interaction in the counseling block I used. The metabolic language is in-vitro CYP1A2 induction.[1] Alcohol, by contrast, has an explicit "prudent to avoid" line. That comparison is on [modafinil and alcohol](/blog/modafinil-and-alcohol).
