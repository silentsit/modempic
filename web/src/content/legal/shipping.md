# Shipping & Handling

## We Ship Worldwide

**100% free shipping** on every order — worldwide express mail. No signature required on delivery.

Prices are in USD. Duties, taxes, or postal fees in your country are the recipient's responsibility.

## International Shipments

**USA, Canada, Australia, UK:** 7 – 14 business days

**South-East Asia:** 4 – 6 business days

**Rest of the world:** 14 – 21 business days

These are estimates. Weather, carrier volume, holidays, and customs can add time.

## Parcels Stuck at Customs

If tracking shows the parcel has been stuck at customs for **14 days**, [email us](/contact) with your order number. We will always try to **resend the order** first, at no extra charge.

If that second shipment also fails to arrive, we will refund the order when you ask. See the [Return Policy](/refund-policy).

## Shipping Process: What Happens After You Order?

Orders are processed within **12 hours** of payment confirmation. We email the tracking number when the parcel goes out.

Tracking often takes about **3 days** to show up on global tracking sites.

If something looks off, [email us](/contact). We reply by email.

## How to Track Your Order

Start with [Post Track](https://posttrack.com/) or [17 Track](https://www.17track.net/). Those two stay the most current for the carriers we use.

Depending on the tracking number:

- **SG** suffix — [SingPost](https://www.singpost.com/track-items)
- **IN** suffix — [India Post](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/_layouts/15/dop.portal.tracking/trackconsignment.aspx)
- **CH** suffix — [Swiss Post](https://www.post.ch/en/online-services/track)
- **FR** suffix — [La Poste](https://www.laposte.fr/outils/track-a-parcel)
- **INAWABET** prefix — DHL India
- **UK deliveries** — [Royal Mail](https://www.royalmail.com/track-your-item)

If the number still does not make sense, send it to [info@modempic.com](mailto:info@modempic.com).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. **What are the shipping options, and how much do they cost?**

2. **How long does processing and delivery take?**  
   Processed within 12 hours. USA, Canada, Australia, and the UK: about 7–14 business days. South-East Asia (Vietnam, Singapore, Indonesia, Philippines, Brunei, Myanmar, Laos, Cambodia, Malaysia): about 4–6 business days. Everywhere else: about 14–21 business days.

3. **Can I track my order?**  
   Yes. Use the tracking number in your dispatch email with Post Track or 17 Track first.

4. **Do you ship internationally?**  
   Yes. **Free shipping** on every international order. No extra paperwork from us. Your country's customs rules still apply.

5. **What if my order is delayed, lost, or damaged?**  
   [Contact us](/contact) with the order number. If it has been stuck at customs for 14 days, we resend the order first. If the reship also fails to arrive, we refund when you ask.

   Email [info@modempic.com](mailto:info@modempic.com).
