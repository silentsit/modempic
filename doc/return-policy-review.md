# Return Policy — review draft

**Status:** review only. Do not treat this as published until you approve it.

**Effective date: 27 August 2026**

This is the return and refund contract for orders on modempic.com. It is part of the [Terms of Service](https://modempic.com/terms-of-service). Shipping rates, tracking, and customs reship are on [Shipping](https://modempic.com/shipping).

Questions: [info@modempic.com](mailto:info@modempic.com). Include your order number and the email used at checkout.

You must be 18 or older to order. Catalog copy is not medical advice.

## What you can return

You may ask to return a product for a refund within **14 days of delivery** (the date tracking shows delivered).

To qualify, the product must be:

- **unopened** — safety seal intact, bottle or pack not opened
- **in the original packaging** — labels and inserts included

Tell us why you are returning it. We use that to check the request, not as a public review.

Sale or promo items follow this same policy unless the offer page states a different return rule.

## What you cannot return

We do not accept:

- opened or unsealed products
- used products
- returns started more than 14 days after delivery
- returns without an order number we can match
- parcels seized, returned, or destroyed by customs as a product return (if the parcel never arrives, see Lost, delayed, or held parcels)

**Exceptions.** We will still put this right if:

- we sent the wrong product
- the product is defective or damaged on arrival

For those exceptions, contact us before using the rest of the order if you can, and keep photos of the parcel and product.

## How to start a return

1. Email [info@modempic.com](mailto:info@modempic.com) or use the [contact page](https://modempic.com/contact) within 14 days of delivery.
2. Send the order number, the email on the order, which lines you want to return, and the reason. Photos help for damage or a wrong item.
3. Wait for our reply. Do not ship anything back until we say so. Unauthorised parcels may be refused.
4. If we approve the return, we send instructions. Where we can, we provide a return label.

**Return shipping.** If the return is because we made an error (wrong or defective item), we pay return shipping. For a change of mind on an unopened product, you pay return shipping unless your local law says otherwise.

**Inspection.** We inspect what comes back. If it does not meet this policy, we may refuse the refund and, where practical, send the product back at your cost.

## Refunds

If we approve the return, we refund the product amount for the lines we accepted.

- Outbound shipping is refunded only if we made an error, or if the whole order is cancelled before it ships, or if your local law requires it.
- Return shipping you paid for a change-of-mind return is not refunded.
- Duties, VAT, or postal fees your country charged are not refunded by us.

**Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay.** Refunds go back through PeptidePay to the original method. Banks can take several business days to show the credit.

**Crypto.** We refund the approved product amount by a method we confirm with you by email, usually the original asset. Network fees are not refunded. Crypto values move; we refund the product amount we charged, not later market price.

Partial returns get a partial refund. Promo discounts are allocated across the order; we will not refund more than you paid for that line.

We aim to start an approved refund within several business days after we inspect the return (or after we agree no return shipment is needed).

## Lost, delayed, or held parcels

Email us with the order number.

- **Stuck at customs.** If the parcel has been stuck at customs for **14 days**, email us with the order number. We will always try to **resend the order** first, at no extra charge. If that second shipment also fails to arrive, we will refund the order when you ask.
- **Lost or damaged in transit.** Contact us. We will check tracking and decide on a reship or refund.
- **Customs seizure or destruction.** That is not a product return. If the parcel never arrives, use the stuck-at-customs steps above (resend first, then a refund if you ask after the reship also fails).

## Cancellations before dispatch

If we have not handed the parcel to the carrier, email us. We will cancel and refund if we can still stop the order. Once it is with the carrier, use the return rules above.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change. An order already placed follows the return rules in force on the order date, except where the law requires a better remedy for you.
