## Quick wins vs long-term

### Quick wins (0–90 days)

1. **Fix URL targeting** — Labs + GSC show homepage ranking for buy/sale queries. Strengthen internal links and canonical signals so `/where-to-buy-modafinil-online` (and armodafinil equivalent) earn those rankings.
2. **Title/H1/meta for "for sale"** — Add Provigil/Nuvigil sale variants to landing copy (GSC: provigil for sale 105 imp, modafinil for sale 279 imp).
3. **Artvigil / Waklert PDPs** — GSC positions ~30 for buy artvigil*; competitors (modafinilxl, modamindfuels) own SERP slots.
4. **Zero-click problem** — Impressions without clicks at pos 30–45 suggest weak snippets. Rewrite meta descriptions with price/shipping/crypto trust hooks.

### Long-term (3–12 months)

1. **Head term "modafinil"** (165k/mo, KD 42) — authority build via comparison guides, dosage, vs adderall (3.6k/mo KD 0).
2. **Modalert 200** (260/mo, KD 45) — dedicated brand guide + PDP.
3. **Modvigil** (590/mo, KD 59) — harder; needs backlinks.
4. **Comparison content** — modalert vs waklert, waklert vs artvigil (KD 4, 50/mo).
