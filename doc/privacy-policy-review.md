# Privacy Policy — review draft

**Status:** review only. Do not treat this as published until you approve it.

**Effective date: 27 August 2026**

This notice explains how the operator of modempic.com (“Modempic,” “we,” “us”) handles personal information when you visit the site, check out as a guest or with an account, or email us.

It applies to modempic.com. It does not apply to PeptidePay, Paymento, or other sites we link to — those companies have their own notices.

Contact: [info@modempic.com](mailto:info@modempic.com).

We are an e-commerce catalog, not a clinic, pharmacy benefit manager, insurer, or bank. We do not treat order data as HIPAA “protected health information.”

## What we collect

**You give us**

- name, email, shipping and billing address, phone
- account password (stored as a hash) if you register
- order notes, support messages, and reviews
- messages you type in the on-site chat

**Orders create**

- products, quantities, prices, payment method, order number, and payment status
- a guest-order cookie so you can open the confirmation page without signing in

We do **not** receive your full card number. PeptidePay (and Apple Pay / Google Pay through that flow) handles card data. Paymento handles crypto checkout.

**The site and device send**

- IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and basic server logs
- cookies described below

**Sign-in providers**

If you use Google, LinkedIn, or Instagram to sign in, we receive the account identifiers that provider shares (typically name and email).

## Why we use it

**Fill the order** — cart, checkout, payment confirmation, shipping, tracking email.

**Account** — sign-in, password reset, order history.

**Support** — replies to [info@modempic.com](mailto:info@modempic.com).

**Security** — fraud checks, blocking abuse, keeping the site up.

**Legal** — tax records, disputes, requests from authorities where required.

**Improve the shop** — see which pages fail, fix bugs, understand which products are viewed.

**Optional marketing** — product or restock emails, only where allowed. You can unsubscribe.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

## Legal bases (EEA / UK)

If you are in the EEA or UK, we use:

- **Contract** — to take and ship your order and to run an account you asked for
- **Legitimate interests** — site security, fraud prevention, basic analytics, guest-cart cookies, and improving the shop, balanced against your rights
- **Consent** — optional marketing emails, and any non-essential cookies if we add a consent banner later
- **Legal obligation** — records we must keep

You can withdraw marketing consent at any time. That does not affect emails about an order you already placed.

## Who we share it with

Only what each vendor needs:

- **PeptidePay** — hosted card / Apple Pay / Google Pay
- **Paymento** — crypto checkout
- **Resend** — transactional email (order, shipping, password reset, and similar)
- **Cloudinary** — product images
- **Vercel** — hosting the website
- **Our database host** — storing accounts, carts, and orders
- **OpenAI** — on-site chat replies (your chat text is sent to generate an answer; do not paste medical records or card numbers into chat)
- **Carriers / posts** — name, address, and phone so the parcel can move
- **Sign-in providers** — if you choose Google, LinkedIn, or Instagram

We may share information if the business is sold, or if the law requires it (court order, subpoena, or similar).

Staff who handle orders can see what they need to pack, refund, or support an order.

## Cookies

- **`modempic_guest_cart`** — guest shopping cart. About 30 days. HttpOnly.
- **`modempic_guest_orders`** — lets a guest open their confirmation page. About 30 days. HttpOnly.
- **Session cookie** (`authjs.session-token` or `__Secure-authjs.session-token`) — signed-in session.
- **Checkout draft in sessionStorage** — remembers address fields in this browser tab until the tab is closed or the order is placed.

These are first-party cookies we set. The guest-cart and guest-order cookies are **httpOnly**.

We do not currently run a third-party advertising pixel network. We do not respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals.

You can block cookies in your browser. If you block the guest-cart cookie, checkout as a guest will not keep a cart.

## How long we keep it

- **Guest cart cookie:** up to 30 days of inactivity
- **Account:** while the account exists, then we delete or anonymize what we no longer need
- **Orders and payment records:** for as long as needed to ship, handle refunds, taxes, fraud, and disputes, then delete or anonymize
- **Support email:** as long as the thread is useful, then in line with the same record rules
- **Chat:** processed to answer you; we do not keep a long-term chat archive in our own database

## Your rights

Email [info@modempic.com](mailto:info@modempic.com). Say which address you ordered with.

Depending on where you live, you may be able to:

- access a copy of your information
- correct it
- delete it (we may keep what the law requires, such as a paid-order record)
- export it
- object to or restrict certain processing
- opt out of marketing
- for California residents: know, delete, and correct personal information, and opt out of “sale” or “sharing” as those words are used in the CPRA (we do not sell personal information for money)

We will not discriminate against you for making a privacy request.

We may need to verify it is you (for example, that you control the order email).

EEA/UK users can also complain to their local data protection authority.

This notice **does** describe legal rights. It does not replace mandatory consumer or privacy statutes.

## International transfers

The site is hosted on Vercel. Order data, email, images, payments, and chat may be processed in the United States and other countries (including where those vendors operate in Asia or Europe).

If you are in the EEA or UK, those transfers rely on the vendor’s published tools (such as standard contractual clauses) where the vendor provides them.

## Children

This shop is for adults. We do not sell to anyone under 18, and we do not aim the site at children. If you think a minor gave us information, email [info@modempic.com](mailto:info@modempic.com) and we will delete what we can.

## Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, httpOnly cookies for guest carts, and access limits on order data. No method of transmission is perfect. You should log out on a shared device.

If we are required to notify you of a breach, we will use the email on the order or account.

## Marketing

Order, shipping, and password emails are transactional. You cannot turn those off and still receive the service.

Promotional emails (if we send them) will include an unsubscribe link.

## Social media

If we run Instagram or other pages, anything you post there is public on that platform. Do not post order numbers or addresses in comments.

## Changes

We will change the effective date when this notice changes in a material way. Continued use of the site after that date means the new notice applies to later visits and orders. For a material change to how we use information we already have, we will use email or an on-site notice where the law requires it.
